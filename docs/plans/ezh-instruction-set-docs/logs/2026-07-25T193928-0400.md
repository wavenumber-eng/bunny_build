# Correction: corpus counts re-run with dedup; the thesis sharpens

My own catalog said "Deduplicate before quoting counts anywhere" and then I
quoted the raw counts in the summary and founded the cookbook thesis on them.
Re-run with content-hash dedup across all four corpora, at file *and* program
level.

## Deduplicated inventory

| | pre-dedup | deduplicated |
|---|---|---|
| EZH-bearing source files | 37 | **26** |
| EZH programs | 146 | **87** |

40% redundancy, worse than a first look at `ezh-old` alone suggests, because
**avc vendors a copy of bunny_build's entire test tree** plus the camera
generator - its 18 apparent programs are 4 distinct ones. Per corpus after dedup:
ezh-old 70, bunny_build 8, gibbon-d 5, avc 4.

Ten duplicate groups, and they are exactly the interesting files: `ezh_features.c`
(two byte-identical 779-line copies), `ezh_bist.c`, `ezh_camera.c` (three copies),
and the three display-controller sources.

real application.

## The park/terminator finding is much stronger than I reported

I said the park block appears in "two programs". Counting occurrences across
distinct files:

| File | park blocks | `E_INT_TRIGGER(0)` |
|---|---|---|
| `ezh_features.c` | 8 | 10 |
| `ezh_flexio_dma.c` (RT500 variant) | 8 | 6 |
| `ezh_flexio_dma.c` (other variant) | 0 | 35 |
| `ezh_flexio_dma.c` (third) | 1 | 4 |
| `ezh_mipi_dma.c` | 0 | 2 |
| `ezh_flexio_mip_dma.c` | 1 | 1 |
| `ezh_bist.c` | 1 | 0 |
| gibbon-d `ezh_app.cpp` | 1 | 0 |
| bunny_build `psuedo_instr.c` | 1 | 0 |

So the park is the corpus's **standard program terminator**, present in seven
distinct sources including third-party production code (gibbon-d), not an idiom
two programs happened to share. bunny_build packaging it is straightforwardly
correct.

**Secondary benefit, and the more valuable one:** `E_INT_TRIGGER(0)` occurs about
**sixty times** across the deduplicated corpus - 35 in a single display-controller
source. The construct AN14650 §4.2.14 describes as out of spec is used ~60 times
in NXP's own sample code. That is countable corroboration for the
`E_INT_TRIGGER(0)` resolution, replacing the chapter's vague "every program in
the sample corpus".

Both chapters updated: the assembler guide's park section now states the real
spread, and the architecture chapter's `int-trigger-zero` basis now cites
measurement rather than impression.

## Process note

The catalog caught the duplication and said so; the summary quoted the numbers
anyway. Writing "deduplicate before quoting" and then quoting is worse than not
noticing, because the caveat makes the number look checked. If a caveat is worth
writing down, it is worth applying before the next paragraph - or the measurement
should not be published until it has been.
